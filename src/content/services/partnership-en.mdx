When an IT company gets a mobile project, it does not need to open an internal mobile department. TuronCore joins as a white-label or technical partner.

## Models

- White-label: your client, our delivery
- Technical partnership: we work with your team
- Short sprints or a long-term partnership

You do not lose mobile projects because of missing capacity. If white-label is agreed, we stay behind your brand.
