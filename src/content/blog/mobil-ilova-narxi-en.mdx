There is no single number for "how much is an app" in Tashkent. Price follows screens, roles, payments, and integrations.

## What is included

The mobile app itself starts at **25 million UZS**. That is usually a scoped MVP: the core flow, iOS and Android (Flutter), basic QA, and store-ready packaging.

Price goes up when you need two roles, payments, maps, real-time chat, a separate admin, or a rewrite of a live app.
