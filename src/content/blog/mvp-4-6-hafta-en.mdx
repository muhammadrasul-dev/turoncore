Four to six weeks is **not a full ecosystem**. It is the first useful flow. Seller + buyer + admin + chat + payments may not all fit.

The MVP includes one core user goal, iOS and Android (usually Flutter), tests for main failures, and a closed beta. Extra push types, heavy analytics, a third role, and a full design system wait for Grow.
